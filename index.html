IDX Exchange

March 2026 to present

Data Analyst Intern in the MLS Analytics Program. I built a housing data pipeline in pandas, from raw monthly files to Tableau dashboards.

  1. Combine. Merged monthly MLS sold and listing files from January 2024 forward into unified datasets.
  2. Filter. Kept residential properties only, with a row-count check at every step.
  3. Enrich. Added national 30-year fixed mortgage rates from the St. Louis Fed (FRED) API, resampling weekly data to monthly averages for a time-series join.
  4. Flag. Marked invalid records (illogical date sequences, negative values, out-of-range coordinates) instead of removing them.
  5. Show. Built Tableau dashboards tracking close price, list price, and days on market for stakeholder-facing housing market reports.
